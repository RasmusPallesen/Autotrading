Four-Layer Architecture

Execution Model
Three-Cadence Loop
Hot path, position sync, and full market sweep operating in parallel
Hot Path · ~5 seconds
WebSocket bar-close events trigger a single-symbol AI decision. Only fires when _is_hot() criteria met: volume ×2, RSI bounce (<30→≥30), VWAP cross, 20-bar high, EMA-21 cross, or held-position drop >2%.
Mini Loop · 60 seconds
Evaluates all currently held positions every 60 seconds. Rapid stop-loss and take-profit monitoring without triggering a full 55-symbol sweep.
Full Sweep · 15 minutes
Complete pass across all 55 symbols enriched with research signals from all 14 data monitors. Discovers new entries and confirms existing positions.
